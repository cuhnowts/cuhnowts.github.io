Personal Finance

Purpose

Personal Finance is a private, local budgeting and cash-flow tracker for its owner. It helps review balances and transaction history, categorize spending, and understand household finances.

When an account connection is enabled, it uses read-only access to retrieve balances and transaction history. It does not initiate payments or transfers, make lending decisions, or retrieve account and routing numbers.

Privacy

This website is informational and does not collect financial data. Financial data obtained through an approved connection is used only by the owner’s local personal-finance tool and is stored on an owner-controlled device. Bank credentials are entered only with the financial institution or its connection provider, never on this website.

Data is not sold or used for advertising. The owner can disconnect an account connection at any time.
